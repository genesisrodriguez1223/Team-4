## 3. Stakeholder List

* **Project Members** – Their Role is to maintain and provide progress towards the end product. Their interest is a fully working project that is delivered in a timely manner with no issues.

* **Manager(s)** - Their role is to approve and provide the necessary resources to the team members to guide them towards the intended project. Their interest is to make sure that the project being worked on is flowing at a steady pace and to ensure the team members understand the overall objective.

* **Users** – Their role serves as the people who use the final product. Their Interest lies in wanting to ensure that what they are using is a product that is reliable and has minimal defects.
